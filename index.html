05

Truss Bridge Load Test

statics · hand calcs vs. measured reactions · team of 2

  • Designed and built a 9-member truss with a partner, then confirmed it was statically determinate (2j = m + r)
  • Set the bridge on two scales and tested two 200 g load cases to measure the support reactions
  • Solved every member force by method of joints and sections; predicted reactions landed within 0.026 N of the measured values on average
  • Traced the remaining error to the as-built geometry (a diagonal that wasn't a perfect 45°) and to rounding carried through the calculations
06

Linkage Motion Analysis

SolidWorks Motion · MATLAB · simulation vs. measured data

  • Modeled a planar linkage in SolidWorks and ran a motion study to get the coupler's path trace and velocities
  • Compared position and velocity data measured on the physical mechanism against the simulation in MATLAB
  • Applied a Butterworth low-pass filter to clean up the noisy measured velocity data
  • Fixed links clipping through each other in the simulation by finding two beams that should have been constrained perpendicular
07

Cantilever Beam FEA

SolidWorks Simulation · static study · hand-calc validation

  • Set up a static FEA study of an end-loaded cantilever beam with fixtures, loads, and material properties
  • Checked the simulation against beam theory: 0.019 in max deflection by hand and in FEA, and 4.5 ksi vs. 4.503 ksi max bending stress
  • Plotted stress along the beam and showed the von Mises stress follows the bending (axial) stress component
  • Compared peak stress with the material’s 37.5 ksi yield strength to check the margin before yielding
