⚙about

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student who's happiest somewhere between a CAD model and a pile of sawdust. I currently work as a Teaching Assistant in Tufts' Materials & Manufacturing Lab, where I help students turn engineering drawings into real parts — sheet metal boxes, sand-cast pins, and machined components — on the drill press, mill, and lathe. Outside the shop, I model and simulate in CAD and COMSOL, and I build the occasional over-engineered LEGO robot.
